The test uses locally transcoded, resized, silent copies with container metadata
removed and neutral filenames. A second local copy prepends 20 seconds of
unrelated blue footage to check late-section recognition. No fixture bytes or
derived videos are shipped in this repository or posted to production.

This fixture shows pasta boiling, not a woman cooking chicken pasta. A passing
worker recognition check must not be described as passing the user's complete
app-search scenario. Actual production upload/index/search remains a separate
acceptance gate after the companion coordinator integration.
